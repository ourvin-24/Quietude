The Geometry of Silence

A continuous visual exploration of solitude, space, and self-reflection. Every drawing in this collection is a journey inward—an attempt to trace the boundaries of personal space using the expressive freedom of scribble art. In a world that is constantly loud, these works serve as visual echoes of silence. The overlapping marks, raw textures, and dense strokes capture the subtle shift from feeling isolated to finding solace in one's own company, transforming the vastness of quiet moments into a canvas of self-discovery.
